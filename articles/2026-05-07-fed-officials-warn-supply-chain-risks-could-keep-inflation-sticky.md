Federal Reserve officials said emerging supply-chain disruptions are adding upside risks to inflation, complicating the path toward interest-rate cuts even as parts of the U.S. economy show signs of cooling.

According to Reuters reporting, policymakers flagged logistics bottlenecks and input-cost pressures as factors that could keep price growth above target for longer than previously expected. The comments come as investors reassess when the Fed may be able to ease policy.

### Why this matters
For businesses and households, persistent inflation can keep borrowing costs higher for longer and delay relief in financing conditions. For markets, shifting Fed expectations can quickly move bond yields, equity valuations, and currency prices.

### What to watch next
Watch upcoming U.S. inflation prints, labor-market data, and Fed speeches for confirmation of whether supply-side pressures are broadening or easing. Any sustained reacceleration in core prices could push expected rate cuts further out.
