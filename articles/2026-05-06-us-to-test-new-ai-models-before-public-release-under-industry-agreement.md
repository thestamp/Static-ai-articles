The U.S. government has reached an agreement with several major AI developers — including Google, Microsoft, and xAI — to allow pre-release safety assessments for certain new models before broader rollout. The framework is positioned as a national-security and public-safety checkpoint in the model deployment pipeline.

Officials and participating companies describe the arrangement as a structured, pre-launch review layer rather than a full licensing regime. Early reporting indicates the process is intended to identify high-risk capability issues before public distribution.

### Why this matters for the technology desk
This is a core model-governance and deployment-policy development: it directly changes how frontier AI systems may move from lab to product, with practical implications for release timelines, risk controls, and competitive strategy.

### What to watch next
- Whether review criteria are published in technical detail.
- Whether additional labs join on the same terms.
- How the process affects release cadence for frontier models in coming quarters.
