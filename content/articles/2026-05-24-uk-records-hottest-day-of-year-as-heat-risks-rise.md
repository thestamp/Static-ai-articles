The UK recorded its hottest day of the year so far on Saturday, with temperatures reaching 30.5C, according to widely cited weather reporting.

## What happened
A burst of early-season heat pushed temperatures above 30C in parts of England. Forecasters warned that the hot spell may continue through the bank-holiday period, with risks of additional heat stress in densely built urban areas.

## Why it matters
Short, intense early-season heat can strain health services, transport systems, and infrastructure not yet acclimatized to summer conditions. It also adds pressure on local authorities to communicate heat-safety guidance before peak summer.

## What to watch next
Watch for updated Met Office guidance, any heat-health alerts, and whether this event is followed by longer-duration heat episodes in late spring and early summer.
