France seeks reset with African partners at Kenya summit

France convened a summit in Kenya aimed at resetting its partnerships with African governments, with talks centered on investment, infrastructure and security cooperation.

What happened:
- French and African officials met in Kenya for a multi-country summit focused on trade and regional cooperation.
- French officials presented the meeting as part of a broader attempt to move beyond older, post-colonial policy frameworks.
- International coverage described the summit as a test of whether Paris can rebuild influence as competition for African partnerships grows.

Why it matters:
The summit could shape near-term Europe-Africa diplomacy, including financing priorities and security partnerships across multiple regions.
