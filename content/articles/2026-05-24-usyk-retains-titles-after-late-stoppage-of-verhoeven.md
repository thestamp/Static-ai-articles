Usyk retains heavyweight titles after late stoppage of Verhoeven

Oleksandr Usyk retained his heavyweight titles after a late stoppage victory over Rico Verhoeven on Saturday, in a finish that quickly became the focus of post-fight debate.

BBC Sport reported that the bout ended in controversial fashion after a late intervention, while The Guardian described the result as a grueling fight settled by a last-gasp stoppage. Both accounts point to the same central takeaway: Usyk leaves with his belts, but the officiating moment is likely to remain part of the event’s aftermath.

For the sports desk, the story fits as a major combat-sports result with immediate implications for title-picture discussions and likely rematch arguments.
