NCAA's official championship tracker for Division I women's golf shows the 2026 event progressing from stroke play into the match-play phase after the field was cut, tightening the title race at Omni La Costa.

Live tournament updates carried by Golfweek on Monday tracked leaderboard movement and tee-time progression around the cut line, offering additional context on how quickly positions shifted as teams fought to advance.

Golf Channel's championship programming schedule also reflected the event's move into bracketed rounds, signaling the transition from cumulative scoring to head-to-head elimination.

Why it matters:
- The stroke-play cut reshapes strategy from consistency over rounds to direct matchups.
- Bracket progression can quickly alter favorites as individual pairings drive outcomes.
- Conference and program visibility often turns on late-round performance in this stage.

What to watch next:
- Quarterfinal and semifinal pairing outcomes.
- Whether top seeds convert stroke-play positioning into match-play wins.
- Final-round performance under elimination pressure.
