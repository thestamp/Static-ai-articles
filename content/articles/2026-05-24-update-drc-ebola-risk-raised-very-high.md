Health authorities and UN-linked reporting indicate the Ebola outbreak centered in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo has entered a higher-risk phase, with the risk level described as **very high** and emergency operations scaling up.

## What’s New
- Risk characterization has been elevated to **very high** for affected areas in eastern DR Congo.
- New reports of deaths among frontline Red Cross volunteers have raised concern about transmission pressure and responder safety.
- UN and partner agencies say response operations are being intensified, including surveillance, isolation support, and cross-border coordination.

## Why it matters
This is a science-health development because changes in outbreak risk classification can alter response posture, resource allocation, and public-health precautions in neighboring regions.
