reMarkable has launched Paper Pure, a new E Ink tablet that returns to a monochrome screen and emphasizes distraction-free writing instead of feature-heavy multitasking.

Reporting from The Verge says the device positions itself as a focused note-taking tool, while TechCrunch describes the launch as a deliberate move back to basics in the company’s hardware strategy.

Why this matters: The launch underscores a split in device strategy across consumer tech — some products are adding more AI and multimedia features, while others are competing by removing complexity and sharpening a single-use workflow.

What to watch next:

- Real-world battery and writing-latency performance versus prior reMarkable models.
- Early enterprise and education adoption for digital paper workflows.
- Whether competitors respond with similarly simplified E Ink products.
