A new wave of Russian missile and drone strikes hit the Kyiv area overnight into Sunday, according to multiple international reports, with fatalities and dozens of injuries reported by local authorities.

BBC reported at least four deaths and dozens injured in what it described as a large-scale attack. AP later reported a subsequent third straight day of heavy attacks that included fatalities in Kyiv and damage to residential buildings.

Officials said emergency crews were deployed across multiple impact sites as air-defense alerts remained active. The strikes come as both sides continue long-range attacks while front-line fighting and diplomatic tensions remain high.

## Why this matters

The renewed strike intensity around the capital highlights how civilian risk remains elevated even far from front lines. For policymakers and aid agencies, repeated mass-casualty strike nights increase pressure on air-defense support, shelter readiness, and emergency medical capacity.
