Voters across England, Scotland and Wales are casting ballots in a broad set of local and devolved contests that analysts view as a major political barometer between general elections.

## What happened
Polling is underway across multiple councils and regional bodies, with results expected to offer an early read on whether governing and opposition parties are consolidating or losing support in different parts of the UK.

## Why it matters
The elections provide one of the clearest near-term measures of public sentiment on leadership, economic management and public services. Party performance in these races can shape strategy, candidate selection and policy positioning ahead of future national contests.

## What to watch next
Watch turnout levels, regional swing patterns and whether any party materially outperforms expectations in areas that recently changed hands.
