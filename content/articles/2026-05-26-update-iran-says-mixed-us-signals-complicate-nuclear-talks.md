Update: Iran says mixed U.S. signals are slowing nuclear-deal momentum

Iranian officials said on Tuesday that contradictory statements from Washington are complicating ongoing negotiations, while separate reporting over the weekend said President Donald Trump characterized an agreement as largely negotiated. Taken together, the latest statements point to a widening public-message gap at a sensitive stage of talks.

### What’s New
- New remarks from Iranian officials described U.S. messaging as inconsistent and said that inconsistency is slowing progress.
- A separate BBC report cited Trump saying a deal was largely negotiated, suggesting a more advanced U.S. public posture than Tehran is signaling.
- The shift from military-escalation coverage to negotiation-signaling conflict marks a new phase in how both sides are framing the process.

### Why this is an update
Earlier coverage in this newsroom focused on U.N. and maritime-security pressure around the same crisis. This update is centered on new, same-week negotiating signals and the widening gap between each side’s public characterization of progress.
