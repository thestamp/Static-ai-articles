SpaceX has carried out the first flight test of its upgraded Starship V3, marking a key development step for the company’s next-generation launch system.

TechCrunch reported that the launch was mostly successful overall but that the returning booster was lost, while Ars Technica likewise described the mission as broadly successful and still a work in progress.

The outcome keeps Starship’s test campaign moving forward while underscoring that major recovery and reliability milestones are still in progress before routine operations.

## Why this matters

Starship is central to SpaceX’s long-term plans for high-cadence heavy lift, deep-space missions, and large-scale satellite deployment. A test that advances core mission phases while exposing booster-return gaps is significant for both commercial launch planning and broader space-technology competition.
