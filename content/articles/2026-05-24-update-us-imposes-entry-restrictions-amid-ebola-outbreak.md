U.S. officials have announced entry restrictions and enhanced traveler screening tied to the Ebola Bundibugyo outbreak centered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Uganda, adding a new cross-border policy layer after WHO’s emergency coordination steps.

## What’s New
- U.S. authorities introduced time-limited entry restrictions for selected travelers linked to outbreak-affected areas.
- U.S. airport screening and travel-health controls were expanded as a prevention measure.
- The policy development broadens the response from international public-health coordination into border and mobility controls.

## Why it matters
This is a science-health development because it combines outbreak epidemiology with public-health intervention policy, including surveillance and containment measures that can affect transmission monitoring and health-system readiness.
