The U.S. measles picture is continuing to worsen, with the CDC's latest national tracker showing additional confirmed cases and ongoing multistate outbreaks. The update adds to evidence that local transmission chains are still active rather than fully contained.

Independent reporting from CIDRAP also points to fresh weekly increases, reinforcing the CDC trendline and suggesting that public-health agencies will need sustained vaccination and contact-tracing efforts through early summer.

Why this matters for the science-health desk:
- Measles is highly transmissible, so small immunity gaps can lead to rapid regional flareups.
- Case growth in multiple jurisdictions increases coordination demands across state and local health systems.
- The trajectory is a live test of post-pandemic routine immunization recovery.

What to watch next:
- Weekly CDC updates for acceleration or plateau signals.
- State-level school and community vaccination campaign data.
- Any federal or state escalation in outbreak response guidance.
