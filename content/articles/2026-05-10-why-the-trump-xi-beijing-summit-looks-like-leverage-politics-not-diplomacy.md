The coming Trump-Xi meeting in Beijing is being cast by analysts as a leverage contest rather than a reset moment.

Commentary published by *The Guardian* argues that Beijing enters the talks with stronger bargaining positions across multiple files, including Taiwan and Iran, while Washington is balancing domestic political pressure with strategic signaling abroad.

For readers, the key near-term question is not whether the summit produces headline-friendly language, but whether either side signals concrete shifts in risk management around flashpoints. If neither side moves beyond positional messaging, expectations for durable agreements should remain limited.
