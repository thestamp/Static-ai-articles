Felix Rosenqvist won the 110th Indianapolis 500 on Sunday, with race coverage from ESPN, INDYCAR, and Indianapolis local outlet WISH-TV all describing a dramatic late-race sprint to the flag.

Those reports characterized the finish as the closest in Indianapolis 500 history, capping a race that turned on late cautions and a short final-run showdown.

INDYCAR's official race report said Rosenqvist prevailed at Indianapolis Motor Speedway after a final restart sequence, while ESPN and WISH-TV similarly framed the result as a last-lap deciding moment.

## Why this matters

The Indianapolis 500 is IndyCar's marquee event and one of the most-watched races in U.S. motorsport. A record-tight finish in the race's 110th running is significant both for the championship narrative and for the series' broader fan momentum early in the season.
